Cookie Policy

How Maploti uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and remember your saved properties.

Last updated: 7 August 2026

1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. The Maploti website also uses browser local storage — a similar, modern technology that keeps small amounts of data on your device so we can remember your preferences between visits.

2. What we store and why

  • Authentication (local storage) — when you sign in, your session token is kept on your device so you stay signed in across pages and visits. This is what makes the "Saved" feature and your profile work.
  • Saved properties (local storage) — the listings you heart are cached locally so the page loads instantly, then synced to your account.
  • Preferences (local storage) — small UI preferences such as your last search area, so returning visitors pick up where they left off.
  • Essential operation — a small number of first-party cookies keep the site working correctly (for example, remembering your session and preferences). We do not currently serve third-party advertising or run cookie-based ad tracking.

We do not use cookies to sell advertising or to build profiles about you for third parties.

3. Third-party services

Maploti runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database and storage) and uses mapping providers to display property locations. These providers may set their own cookies or store data on your device to provide their services. Their use of data is governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to review.

4. Managing or deleting cookies

You can clear cookies and site data at any time through your browser settings (usually under Privacy & Security → Clear browsing data). Doing so may sign you out and clear your local preferences, but will not delete the data saved to your Maploti account.

In the mobile app, no third-party cookies are used — your session is handled securely by the app itself.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the latest version.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies or local storage? Email hello@maploti.app or write to Maploti, Nairobi, Kenya.
