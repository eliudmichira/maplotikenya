FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers

Is Maploti really free to use?

Yes. Browsing, searching, saving favourites and contacting owners or agents costs nothing. There are no viewing fees, no hidden charges and no subscriptions for buyers and renters.

How do I know a listing is genuine?

Every listing shows real photos, exact specs and the owner or agent behind it. Verified listings carry a badge, and because you contact the seller directly on WhatsApp or phone, you can confirm everything before you visit.

How do I contact the owner or agent?

Open any listing and tap Call or WhatsApp — it goes straight to the person who listed it. No forms, no waiting, no middlemen passing your details around.

How do I list my own property?

Create a free account, then publish straight to the map — photos, price, location and specs in minutes. Your listing goes live instantly on the website and the mobile app.

Do you charge owners or agents?

Listing a property on Maploti is free. We may introduce optional promotional features in future, but the core platform stays free for everyone.
